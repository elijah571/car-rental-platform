By checking the acceptance box and proceeding with a car rental, the customer (“Renter”) confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to the following terms and conditions.

1. Parties to the Agreement

This Agreement is between [Company Legal Name] (“Company”) and the individual or organization renting a vehicle through the Company's car rental platform (“Renter”).

This Agreement applies to each rental booking made through the platform.

2. Eligibility and Driver Requirements

The Renter agrees that:

1. They must provide accurate, complete, and truthful personal information.
2. They must hold a valid driver's licence appropriate for the rented vehicle and comply with all applicable legal requirements.
3. They must provide any identification, licence verification, or other documentation reasonably required by the Company.
4. They must not allow any person who is not authorized by the Company to drive the vehicle.
5. They must promptly notify the Company if their licence becomes invalid, suspended, or restricted during the rental period.

The Company may refuse to release a vehicle if the Renter does not meet the applicable eligibility or verification requirements.

3. Booking and Rental Period

1. The Renter must select the vehicle, rental start time, rental end time, and any other required booking details before confirming a reservation.
2. The rental period begins and ends at the times stated in the confirmed booking, subject to any agreed adjustment.
3. The Renter is responsible for checking that the vehicle details, rental period, and displayed price are correct before completing payment.
4. A booking is not considered paid or confirmed until the platform receives and verifies the required payment, unless the Company explicitly confirms otherwise.
5. An unpaid booking may be held for up to 15 minutes. If payment is not completed within that period, the temporary reservation may expire and the vehicle may be made available to other customers.
6. A booking confirmation does not authorize the Renter to collect or drive the vehicle before the Company has completed the required handover procedures.

4. Rental Fees and Payment

1. The rental rate is the vehicle-specific hourly rate expressly displayed and agreed to for the booking at checkout.
2. Rental charges are calculated using the applicable hourly rate and the rental duration recorded by the platform.
3. The platform calculates the final charge on a proportional basis for the rental duration and rounds the amount to the nearest US cent.
4. All rental prices and payments are denominated in United States dollars (USD).
5. The Renter authorizes the platform's designated payment provider to process the payment required for the booking.
6. The Renter must not attempt to manipulate the booking duration, payment process, or payment verification system.
7. Any additional charges must be disclosed to the Renter and applied only where authorized by the applicable booking terms and law.

The Company will provide a booking or payment confirmation when the required payment has been successfully verified.

5. Vehicle Condition and Handover

Before accepting the vehicle, the Renter agrees to:

1. Review the vehicle's make, model, colour, registration number, and other identifying details.
2. Participate in or review the pre-rental inspection where required.
3. Report visible damage, missing equipment, warning lights, or other concerns before driving away.
4. Confirm receipt of the vehicle in the condition recorded in the handover inspection, subject to any documented exceptions.
5. Follow the Company's reasonable vehicle handover and return procedures.

The Renter should retain the rental confirmation and any inspection report provided by the Company.

6. Responsible Use of the Vehicle

The Renter agrees to:

1. Operate the vehicle safely and in accordance with applicable traffic laws.
2. Use the vehicle only for lawful purposes.
3. Keep the vehicle reasonably secure when unattended.
4. Use appropriate safety equipment and ensure that all occupants comply with applicable safety requirements.
5. Take reasonable care to prevent theft, loss, and avoidable damage.
6. Notify the Company promptly if a mechanical fault, warning light, or safety concern arises.
7. Follow any reasonable operating instructions supplied with the vehicle.

The Renter must not:

Use the vehicle for an unlawful activity or unauthorized commercial purpose.
Drive under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, or any substance that makes driving unsafe.
Use the vehicle for racing, reckless driving, or prohibited off-road activities.
Sublet, lend, sell, or transfer possession of the vehicle without the Company's written authorization.
Permit an unauthorized person to drive the vehicle.
Tamper with the odometer, tracking equipment, safety systems, or other vehicle equipment.
Use the vehicle in a way that violates applicable law or the Company's disclosed restrictions.

7. Accidents, Theft, Damage, and Emergencies

If the vehicle is involved in an accident, theft, attempted theft, or other serious incident, the Renter must, as reasonably possible:

1. Prioritize the safety of all people involved and contact emergency services when necessary.
2. Notify the Company promptly.
3. Contact the appropriate authorities where legally required.
4. Provide truthful information and reasonable cooperation for incident reporting and investigation.
5. Avoid making unauthorized admissions of liability or agreeing to settlements on behalf of the Company.
6. Preserve relevant evidence and provide available photographs, reports, and other requested information.

The Renter may be responsible for loss or damage to the extent established under this Agreement, the applicable insurance arrangements, and governing law. No liability under this clause is intended to override mandatory legal protections or valid insurance coverage.

8. Fuel, Cleaning, Fines, and Other Charges

Unless the booking states otherwise:

1. The Renter must return the vehicle in the condition required by the Company's disclosed return policy, allowing for reasonable wear and tear.
2. The Renter is responsible for traffic fines, tolls, parking charges, and similar costs attributable to their use of the vehicle, to the extent permitted by law.
3. The Renter may be charged for additional cleaning, missing equipment, or damage only where the charge is supported by the applicable policy and appropriate evidence.
4. The Company must provide an explanation and reasonable supporting documentation for disputed damage-related charges.

Any applicable fuel, cleaning, deposit, insurance, or additional-driver conditions must be disclosed separately before the Renter becomes bound by them.

9. Late Return and Extension Requests

1. The Renter must return the vehicle by the agreed end time and follow the Company's return instructions.
2. A request to extend the rental must be submitted before the scheduled end time and is subject to Company approval and vehicle availability.
3. Submitting an extension request does not itself extend the booking.
4. The Renter must not assume that additional rental time has been approved until the Company confirms it.
5. Any additional rental charges must be calculated according to the applicable disclosed pricing terms.
6. If the Renter cannot return the vehicle on time due to an emergency or circumstances beyond their control, they must notify the Company as soon as reasonably possible.

The Company may take lawful steps to recover a vehicle that is not returned as agreed.

10. Cancellation, Changes, and Refunds

1. Booking cancellation and modification requests must be submitted through the platform or the Company's designated support channel.
2. Under the platform's current operating procedure, cancellation must be processed by an authorized administrator or staff member.
3. A cancellation request is not effective until the Company confirms that the booking has been cancelled.
4. Refund eligibility, timing, and amount are subject to the disclosed refund policy, the payment provider's procedures, and applicable law.
5. The Company will not represent a refund as completed until the payment provider confirms the relevant transaction outcome.

Nothing in this section excludes any cancellation or refund rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

11. Vehicle Return and Inspection

1. The Renter must return the vehicle to the agreed location and follow the Company's return instructions.
2. The Renter must return all keys, accessories, documents, and other items supplied with the vehicle.
3. The Company or its authorized representative will conduct a return inspection and document the vehicle's condition.
4. The Renter should remain available for the inspection where reasonably possible and may provide information about any incident or condition affecting the vehicle.
5. The Company will compare the return condition with the recorded pre-rental condition, taking reasonable wear and tear into account.
6. The Renter may be asked to review or acknowledge the inspection record. A refusal to sign does not, by itself, establish liability for damage.
7. A vehicle will not be marked available for another rental until the rental has ended and the authorized return inspection has been completed.
8. Where damage or a safety issue is identified, the Company may keep the vehicle unavailable until the matter has been assessed and the vehicle is considered safe and suitable for rental.

12. Insurance and Liability

The Renter must comply with the insurance conditions and coverage limitations disclosed for the booking.

The Company will explain any applicable insurance excess, deductible, exclusions, or additional protection options before the Renter agrees to them.

Nothing in this Agreement:

Removes liability where doing so would be unlawful.
Guarantees insurance coverage for an event that is excluded under the applicable policy.
Makes the Renter automatically responsible for every loss regardless of fault, evidence, or applicable law.

The Renter should contact the Company if they need clarification about the insurance arrangements before accepting the vehicle.

13. Privacy and Records

The Company may process personal, identity, licence, booking, payment-status, and inspection information as reasonably necessary to operate the rental service, prevent fraud, provide customer support, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Personal information must be handled in accordance with the Company's Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection law.

The platform may retain records of the Renter's acceptance of this Agreement, including the agreement version, acceptance timestamp, and associated booking reference, for legitimate operational, security, and legal purposes.

Payment card details should be handled by the designated payment provider in accordance with applicable payment-security requirements.

14. Suspension and Refusal of Service

The Company may refuse to release a vehicle, suspend a booking, or restrict access to the platform where reasonably necessary because of suspected fraud, failed payment, unmet driver requirements, a safety concern, or a material breach of these terms.

Where appropriate and lawful, the Company will provide an explanation and instructions for resolving the issue.

15. Disputes and Governing Law

The parties should first attempt to resolve disputes through the Company's designated customer-support process.

If a dispute cannot be resolved informally, it will be handled under the applicable laws and dispute-resolution procedures of the jurisdiction identified at the beginning of this Agreement, subject to any mandatory consumer-protection rights.
